The Artist

A Stroll Through
the Bible

For over forty years, Richard Pierro has devoted himself to faith and art. He has walked the streets of Jerusalem, stood at the Western Wall, and visited the sites where scripture came to life. That lived pilgrimage breathes soul into every brushstroke.

A Stroll Through the Bible is the result: twenty original paintings, each paired with scripture, installed in Rick's home gallery in Swampscott, Massachusetts. It is not a museum. It is an invitation.

FAQ

What is Christelations?

Christelations is a work of evangelical art. Twenty original paintings by Richard Pierro, each paired with scripture, make the stories of the Bible visible, tangible, and emotionally present. It is not a gallery for art's sake. It is a ministry: an open invitation to walk through the Word and encounter the living God.
